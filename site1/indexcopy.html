Find locations of resources here to help preserve the pollinator species of Montreal

Without pollinators, growing crops, fruits and flowers, which are critical resources on which rely humans and many other species, would be much more difficult that it is now. However, urban expansion and the fragmentation of ecosystems have been threatening our little friends. As of today we are witnessing an alarming decline in animal and vegetal species especially among bees and other pollinators.

This map is for people who'd like to flourish their gardens while making them more suitable for pollinators.

What you can do here: Locate grainotheques where you can get pollinator-attractive plant's seeds and gradening tools for free. You don't own a garden ? No worries, this map will show you all the spots nearby where you can garden.
